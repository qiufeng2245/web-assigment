MamaMiya is more than a music store. We bring carefully selected instruments, honest guidance and a shared love of music to every musician—from their first note to the stage.

01 / Our Story

Music begins with connection.

We started MamaMiya with one simple belief: choosing an instrument should feel personal, inspiring and never intimidating.

Since 2016, we have helped beginners, students and professional performers find instruments that suit their sound and ambition. Every item in our collection is selected for its quality, character and ability to grow with the musician who owns it.
